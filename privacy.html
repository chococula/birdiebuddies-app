BirdieBro Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

BirdieBro is a golf scorecard with a course map. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device location to show where you are on the course map and to measure distances to the green. Your live position stays on your device and is never stored on our servers or shared with other players.

When you start a round without choosing a course, the app looks up the golf course around you by sending your approximate position to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service. When a course is selected, the course's own location is sent instead.

Compass

The map can show which way your phone is pointing. The compass reading is used on your device only and is never sent anywhere.

Anonymous ID

The app signs in to Google Firebase anonymously, which gives this install a random ID. It is used only to know which phone organizes a tournament. No name, email or phone number is attached to it, and deleting the app removes it.

Scores and player names

Player names, handicaps, scores and the chosen course are saved on your device.

Each new round gets a short game code so friends can join and edit scores together. While a round is shared, its course, player names, handicaps and scores are stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database. Anyone who has the game code can view and change that round. Do not enter information you would not want the people you play with to see.

Live leaderboard. If you share a round's live leaderboard link, anyone with that link can view (but not change) the player names and scores of that round.

Tournaments. A tournament stores its name, course and the game codes of the groups that joined, plus the organizer's anonymous ID. Anyone with the tournament code can view the combined leaderboard; only the organizer can rename it or remove groups.

Green positions. Green pins you place on the map are shared, without any player information, so other golfers at the same course see them.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database stores shared rounds, tournaments and green pins; Firebase Authentication issues the anonymous ID (Firebase privacy).
  • OpenGolfAPI receives course names you search for, and your position when you search for nearby courses.
  • Esri and OpenStreetMap provide map tiles; like any website, they receive the map area being viewed. Map tiles for a course are saved on your device so the map works without signal.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass provides hole layouts for the course location described above.

Children

BirdieBro is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device, and History can be cleared inside the app. To delete a shared round from our database, send us its game code at the contact below.
